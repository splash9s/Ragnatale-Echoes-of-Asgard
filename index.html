Element System

Each element beats one other element. Fire beats Wind, Wind beats Earth, Earth beats Ice, and Ice beats Fire.

  • Attacking with the element that beats the target deals x1.25 damage.
  • Attacking with the element the target beats deals x0.75 damage.
  • Same or no element deals x1 damage.
  • Weapons can roll one element (40% chance). Campaign monsters take their path element.
  • Void Rune ignores all monster elements.
🔥 Fire Path: beats Wind
🌪️ Wind Path: beats Earth
🪨 Earth Path: beats Ice
💧 Ice Path: beats Fire

Campaign Structure

4 paths, each with 11 maps (Lv. 1, 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 70, 80, 90, 99). Stage counts: Lv. 1 and 10 have 11, Lv. 20 has 15, Lv. 30 and 40 have 16, Lv. 50, 60 and 70 have 21, Lv. 80 and 90 have 26, Lv. 99 has 31. The Final Map (Lv. 110, 25 stages) unlocks after Lv. 99 is cleared on all four paths.

  • Stage 7: Mini Boss. Last stage: Boss (Champion).
  • Rune offers after stages 1, 3, 6, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, then every 5th stage.
  • The first clear of each map awards 1-5 Diamond.
  • Each path keeps its own progress. The next map unlocks after the previous one is cleared.

Elemental Forge Rules

  • Pick a weapon and an Elemental Stone, then forge the element onto the weapon.
  • Each forge costs Gold = item level x 100, and Elemental Stones = item level / 10 (rounded up, at least 1).
  • Forging succeeds 20% of the time.
  • A failed forge never loses the weapon. It only uses up the stone and the Gold.
  • A forged weapon is named after you: your name in blue, then the element, then the weapon name.
  • Each campaign boss drops the Elemental Stone of its element, with a 5% chance per kill.
  • Weapons no longer roll an element when they drop from monsters.

Refine Rules

  • Each refine costs (current level + 1) × 100 Gold and 1 Refining Stone.
  • +0 to +4: refining always succeeds.
  • From +5 on, the success rate drops by 15% per level (minimum 10%).
  • A failed refine drops the gear by 1 level (never below +4) and consumes the Gold and the Refining Stone.
  • Maximum refinement is +10.
  • Refining Stones: 100 Iron Scraps smelt into 1 Refining Stone.
