# Ragnatale: Lore

Long ago, the gods of Asgard tied the world together with a single golden thread called the Ragnatale. As long as it held, the realms of fire, water, earth, and wind stayed in balance.

Then the Dusk came. Odin's ring cracked, the Ragnatale began to fray, and the World-Eater woke beneath the roots of Yggdrasil, hungry for the threads that hold the world together.

You are a Wanderer from Valgrove, the last town on the edge of the known world. Villagers say that anyone who can gather the shards of the old oaths, earn the gods' favor, and face the World-Eater can mend the thread before the final dawn.

Four paths lie before you:
- **Fire Path**: scorched sands and ember halls
- **Water Path**: drowned shrines and tidal caves
- **Earth Path**: moss-covered ruins and stone halls
- **Wind Path**: high cliffs and storm-torn skies

Along each path, you will meet Slimes, the wobbly jelly spirits that gather where the Ragnatale has worn thin, and the restless creatures of the Dusk. Gather relics, rise in your Class, and forge gear worthy of the gods. Each stage you clear pushes the Dusk back a little further.

Ashmere and Runeholt await beyond the forest and the academy. Whether you are remembered as a hero or forgotten as a shadow depends on how far you dare to go.
